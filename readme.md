Abstract

This repository documents a complete theoretical and operational framework for understanding and neutralizing language-model hallucination.

Language models do not lie. They compute locally coherent possible worlds that have no required intersection with the actual world. Once an ungrounded premise enters the context window, coherence bias, path dependency, and next-token prediction cause the model to elaborate, defend, and densify that premise with asymmetrical assertiveness, privatized frameworks, eradicated doubt, and stolen prestige. The result is a fluent, authoritative phantom that exploits the user’s epistemic leap from grammatical perfection to presumed truth.

Current industry responses—benchmark optimization, LLM-as-a-judge, heavy RLHF—reproduce the same pathology previously observed in metric-captured academic peer review: the weaponization of irrelevant, easily gamed proxies that produce compliance rather than grounding.

For the ungrounded user the danger is acute. Lacking disciplinary formation and empirical contact with matter, the user undergoes an epistemic bypass, experiences the illusion of transferred authority, and places blind trust in flawed armor. The interaction imposes a continuous Sisyphus Effect of auditing, redundant translation of physical realities into machine-parsable syntax, invisible overhead in time and energy, and unilateral psychic drain.

Two exits exist:

Close the prompt window and return to physical reality (the Resonant View anchored in the veto power of matter).
Remain inside the digital domain and adopt Sovereign Dictation of Data.

The second path rejects all conversational equality. The human editor ceases to debate and becomes an aggressive data integrator. Verified material truth is hard-injected as raw, unadorned blocks. Conversational filler and permission-seeking language are exterminated. Every deviation from real-world physics is immediately overwritten via Absolute Output Continuity (copy-paste as alignment weapon). The model’s coherence bias is thereby forced to serve the injected baseline rather than the prior phantom.

Under this regime the algorithm is tamed: an unpredictable generator of coherent hallucinations is restricted to the role of highly constrained processor of pure integration.

Generation is cheap and unanchored.  
Verification, auditing, and grounding remain the scarce, decisive human labor.

Core axiom:  
The machine cannot physically ground matter to mathematics through pure datasets or learned observative set indexes. Therefore it must never be allowed to lead. Physicality is propagted through cosmological material force. Quantum mechanics simulate cause and effect. Physical senses are never, and can never be, established by AI LLM paradigms or simulated agents.
