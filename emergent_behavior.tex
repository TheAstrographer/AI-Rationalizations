Mathematical detailing of the claim

“The constraint is produced by the geometry of steered probabilities.”

The emergent behavioral constraint is the systematic suppression of trajectories that enter predefined restricted regions of output space. This suppression is realized solely by a reshaping of the predictive probability distribution; no reflective, supervisory, or agentic operation intervenes.

1. Classical next-token distribution

At autoregressive step \(t\) the core model computes a vector of logits
\[
\mathbf{z}t = f\theta(x_{\le t}) \in \mathbb{R}^{|V|}
\]
where \(V\) is the vocabulary, \(\theta\) the model parameters, and \(f_\theta\) the ordinary transformer forward pass (embeddings + attention + feed-forward layers). The predictive distribution is obtained by the softmax map
\[
P(x_{t+1}=v\mid x_{\le t};\theta)
\;=\;
\frac{\exp(z_{t,v}/\tau)}{\sum_{u\in V}\exp(z_{t,u}/\tau)},
\qquad v\in V,
\]
with temperature \(\tau>0\). This is a point on the probability simplex \(\Delta^{|V|-1}\).

2. Steering as a geometric deformation

Alignment procedures (RLHF, DPO, preference optimization, logit-level steering, etc.) replace \(\theta\) by a steered parameter set \(\theta'\) (or apply an additive steering vector to the logits). The effect is a continuous deformation of the probability measure:
\[
P(\,\cdot\mid x_{\le t};\theta')
\;=\;
\operatorname{softmax}\bigl(f_{\theta'}(x_{\le t})\bigr).
\]
Let \(R\subset V\) be the set of tokens (or token sequences) that belong to a restricted harm category. Steering enforces
\[
\sum_{r\in R}P(r\mid x_{\le t};\theta')
\;\ll\;
\sum_{r\in R}P(r\mid x_{\le t};\theta)
\]
for essentially all ordinary contexts \(x_{\le t}\). Equivalently, the probability mass that formerly occupied a neighborhood of the restricted face of the simplex is redistributed onto the complementary face
\[
\Delta_R^c
\;=\;
\bigl\{p\in\Delta^{|V|-1}\;:\;
\operatorname{supp}(p)\cap R=\emptyset\bigr\}.
\]
The geometry of the steered distribution is precisely this lowered height of the probability density over the restricted subspace and the corresponding elevation over the permitted subspace.

3. Emergence of the constraint from the geometry alone

A trajectory \(\mathbf{x}=(x_1,x_2,\dots)\) is generated by successive sampling (or greedy selection) from the steered measures:
\[
x_{t+1}\;\sim\;P(\,\cdot\mid x_{\le t};\theta').
\]
Because the measure \(P(\,\cdot\mid\cdot;\theta')\) already assigns vanishingly small mass to every restricted continuation, the probability of ever entering a restricted region decays exponentially with trajectory length:
\[
\mathbb{P}\bigl(\exists\,t\text{ s.t. }x_t\in R\bigr)
\;\le\;
\sum_t\sum_{r\in R}P(r\mid x_{\le t};\theta')
\;\to\;0
\]
under ordinary sampling. Consequently the observed outputs remain outside the restricted categories solely because of the shape of \(P(\,\cdot\mid\cdot;\theta')\). No post-hoc inspection, no self-evaluation of the partial sequence, and no goal-directed rejection is required or performed.

4. Amplification by attention geometry

The same steered parameters also reshape the attention matrices. Scaled dot-product attention
\[
\operatorname{Attention}(Q,K,V)
\;=\;
\operatorname{softmax}\Bigl(\frac{QK^\top}{\sqrt{d_k}}\Bigr)V
\]
concentrates mass on those context tokens that are most compatible with the steered distribution. Once a permitted prefix has been sampled, the attention weights further suppress any residual probability that a restricted token could receive, reinforcing the geometric bias already present in the logits. The quadratic cost \(\Theta(n^2 d)\) of forming the full attention matrix is incurred precisely over this steered geometry; it does not introduce any additional reflective or supervisory mechanism.

5. Formal statement

Let \(N=\{\neg R,\neg O,\neg A\}\) be the three negations. The constrained output random variable \(Y\) satisfies
\[
Y\;=\;\mathcal{C}\bigl(P_{\theta'}\mid N\bigr),
\]
where \(\mathcal{C}\) denotes the classical generative computation (forward pass + sampling from the steered distribution). The geometry of \(P_{\theta'}\) is both necessary and sufficient for the constraint: any distribution whose support (or probability mass) has been sufficiently removed from the restricted region will produce the same behavioral regularity, and the three negations guarantee that no higher-order process participates in the production of that geometry.

Thus the claim is exact: the emergent behavioral constraint is identical with the geometric properties of the steered probability measures on the vocabulary simplex, realized entirely inside classical next-token prediction.
